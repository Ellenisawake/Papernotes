# Referring video object segmentation (RVOS)
### ReflexTrack: A Feedback-Driven Agent for Training-Free Referring Video Object Segmentation
- [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24098)
- University of Surrey, Jiangnan University
- training-free, feedback-driven
- Mask-guided Spatial Refinement evaluates the mask induced by the current keyframe prompt and iteratively updates the bounding box together with positive and negative points
- assesses the complete mask sequence, localizes unreliable intervals, selects complementary repair keyframes, and generates candidate predictions through mask-guided re-propagation

# Referring multi-object tracking (RMOT)
### CD-RMOT-Bench: Benchmarking the Cross-Domain Referring Multi-Object Tracking
- [paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.25239)
- Tianjin University, Shenzhen University of Advanced Technology
- CD-RMOT-Bench, a unified benchmark that combines
  - real clear-domain referring tracking data
  - aligned digital-twin variants, and real adverse-domain videos
- controlled weather/viewpoint shift analysis and realistic synthetic-real transfer evaluation
- Query-Centric Adaptation (QCA) framework
